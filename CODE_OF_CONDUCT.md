# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

CampusPlug aims to be a welcoming, respectful, and useful project for everyone who uses it or contributes to it. We want people with different backgrounds, experience levels, identities, and perspectives to be able to participate without harassment or intimidation.

Everyone who participates in CampusPlug spaces—including issues, pull requests, code reviews, discussions, and other project-related spaces—is expected to help make them safe and constructive.

## Expected behavior

Please:
- Be respectful, considerate, and constructive.
- Assume good intent where reasonable, while taking the impact of your words and actions seriously.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Explain disagreements calmly and remain open to feedback.
- Respect people's privacy. Do not publish personal information without permission.
- Keep discussions relevant and make room for people with different levels of experience.
- Follow project rules and the terms of the platforms where CampusPlug is hosted.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:
- Harassment, threats, intimidation, or sustained personal attacks.
- Discriminatory or degrading language, including slurs.
- Sexualized comments or unwanted advances.
- Publishing private information, or threatening to publish it, without consent.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions or repeatedly targeting another participant.
- Trolling, insults, or other conduct that makes participation hostile.
- Retaliating against someone for raising a concern in good faith.

This list is illustrative, not exhaustive.

## Maintainer responsibilities and enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove or edit inappropriate content, close discussions, restrict participation, or take other action they consider appropriate to protect the community.

When reviewing a report, maintainers will aim to understand what happened, consider the context, protect the privacy of the people involved, and act fairly. Maintainers may take action even if behavior is not explicitly listed above.

## Reporting a concern

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact a project maintainer through their GitHub profile or another contact method they have published. For sensitive concerns, do not post personal details in a public issue. Include links, relevant context, and any evidence you are comfortable sharing. You do not need to confront the person involved.

For security vulnerabilities, use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting feature if it is enabled for this repository, rather than a public issue.

Maintainers should handle reports discreetly and share details only with people who need them to respond. Response times may vary; no specific response deadline is promised.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in CampusPlug project spaces and when someone is representing the project in public spaces. It also applies when project-related conduct outside those spaces has a meaningful impact on the safety or participation of the community.

## Attribution

This policy is adapted from the community standards and structure of the Contributor Covenant. It is intended to express the expectations for the CampusPlug community; it does not replace applicable platform policies or laws.
